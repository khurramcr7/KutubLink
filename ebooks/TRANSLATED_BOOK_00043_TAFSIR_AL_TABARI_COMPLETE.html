2. Complete Scholarly English Translation in Imām al-Ṭabarī's Voice (الترجمة الإنجليزية المحققة الكاملة):
The Discourse on the Exegetical Interpretation (Taʾwīl) of the Statement of the Exalted: ﴿That [narrative] is from the momentous tidings of the Unseen which We reveal by inspiration unto you [O Muḥammad]; neither you nor your people knew it before this [Qur'an]. So persevere patiently; indeed, the blessed final outcome belongs to the God-conscious (49)﴾.

Abū Jaʿfar [Imām al-Ṭabarī] said: The Exalted—Glorified is His mention—declares unto His Prophet Muḥammad ﷺ: This narrative which I have informed you of—concerning the story of Nūḥ (Noah), his account, and the account of his people—is (from the momentous tidings of the Unseen), meaning: it belongs to the hidden accounts of the Unseen which you did not witness in person such that you could know them thereby [1]; (We reveal it unto you), meaning: We Ourselves reveal it unto you by inspiration and thereby make it known to you; (neither you nor your people knew it before this), meaning: before this revelation which We inspire unto you; (So persevere patiently) in steadfastly upholding the command of Allah, conveying His message, and enduring the harm you encounter from the polytheists of your people, just as Nūḥ persevered patiently; (indeed, the blessed final outcome belongs to the God-conscious), meaning: indeed, the ultimate good in the final outcome of affairs belongs to whoever fears and is mindful of Allah [2], fulfills His obligatory duties, and shuns disobedience to Him—for they are the triumphant ones who attain the eternal bliss they hope for in the Hereafter and victory in their quest in this world, just as the final outcome for Nūḥ, when he persevered patiently for the command of Allah, was that Allah delivered him from destruction along with those who believed in him, granted him in the Hereafter what He granted him of honor and noble station, and drowned those who denied him, utterly destroying them all together.
