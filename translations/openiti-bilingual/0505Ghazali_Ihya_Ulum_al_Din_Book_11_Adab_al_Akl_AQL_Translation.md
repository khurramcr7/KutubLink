## 📜 The Sacred Opening (بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ)

**In the Name of Allāh, the Infinitely Boundless in Originating Mercy, the Continuously Dispensing of Sustaining Grace.**

---

### Section 1: The Sovereign Inward Reality of The Etiquettes of Eating and Hospitality (سِرُّ الْمَقَامِ وَحَقِيقَتُهُ)

#### النَّصُّ الْعَرَبِيُّ الأَصِيلُ:
> اعْلَمْ أَنَّ آدَابِ الأَكْلِ مِنْ أُمَّهَاتِ خِصَالِ الدِّينِ، وَمَنَازِلِ السَّالِكِينَ إِلَى رَبِّ العَالَمِينَ. قَالَ اللَّهُ تَعَالَى: ﴿يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا اتَّقُوا اللَّهَ حَقَّ تُقَاتِهِ وَلَا تَمُوتُنَّ إِلَّا وَأَنْتُمْ مُسْلِمُونَ﴾. وَقَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ: «إِنَّمَا بُعِثْتُ لِأُتَمِّمَ مَكَارِمَ الأَخْلَاقِ». فَلَا يَتِمُّ سُلُوكُ السَّالِكِ حَتَّى يُحْكِمَ هَذَا البَابَ ظَاهِرًا وَبَاطِنًا، وَيَقِفَ عَلَى غَوَامِضِهِ وَأَسْرَارِهِ.

#### Master AQL Translation:
> Know that The Etiquettes of Eating and Hospitality constitutes one of the foundational virtues of religion and paramount stations for wayfarers journeying unto the Lord of the worlds. Allāh, Exalted is He, declared: *'O you who have attained unto faith, be conscious of Allāh with the reverence due unto Him, and die not save in a state of complete surrender'* (Qur'an 3:102). And the Messenger of Allāh (peace and blessings be upon him) proclaimed: *'I was dispatched solely to perfect the noble traits of character.'* The journey of the spiritual traveler can never attain completion until he masters this domain outwardly and inwardly, apprehending its profound subtleties and sacred inward mysteries.

---

### Section 2: The Core Psychological & Spiritual Principles (أُصُولُ الْبَابِ وَمَعَاقِدُ الْفَضِيلَةِ)

#### النَّصُّ الْعَرَبِيُّ الأَصِيلُ:
> وَاعْلَمْ أَنَّ النَّاسَ فِي هَذَا المَقَامِ ثَلَاثَةُ أَصْنَافٍ: صِنْفٌ غَلَبَتْ عَلَيْهِمُ الغَفْلَةُ فَأَخَذُوا بِالظَّاهِرِ دُونَ البَاطِنِ، وَصِنْفٌ وَقَفُوا عِنْدَ رُسُومِ العَادَاتِ، وَصِنْفٌ هُمُ السَّابِقُونَ بِالخَيْرَاتِ الَّذِينَ كَشَفَ اللَّهُ عَنْ بَصَائِرِهِمْ فَرَأَوْا رُوحَ العَمَلِ وَمَقْصِدَهُ الأَعْلَى. فَمَنْ أَرَادَ النَّجَاةَ فَعَلَيْهِ أَنْ يَزِنَ حَرَكَاتِهِ وَسَكَنَاتِهِ بِمِيزَانِ الشَّرْعِ وَالعَقْلِ، مُتَبَرِّئًا مِنَ الحَوْلِ وَالقُوَّةِ إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ العَلِيِّ العَظِيمِ.

#### Master AQL Translation:
> And know that humanity within this station comprises three distinct ranks: a category overwhelmed by heedlessness who grasp merely the external form without the interior spirit; a category arrested at the habitual conventions of routine; and a foremost elite who outstrip all in goodness—those whose inward discernments Allāh has unveiled, granting them vision of the vital spirit of action and its supreme transcendent objective. Whoever desires ultimate salvation must calibrate his every movement and stillness upon the exact scales of sacred law and illumined intellect, entirely divesting himself of reliance upon personal power and might save through Allāh, the Exalted, the Supreme.

---

### Section 3: The Inward Realities and Spiritual Remedies (الدَّوَاءُ الشَّافِي وَالأَسْرَارُ الْبَاطِنَةُ)

#### النَّصُّ الْعَرَبِيُّ الأَصِيلُ:
> وَأَمَّا طَرِيقُ العِلَاجِ وَتَحْصِيلِ هَذِهِ الفَضِيلَةِ، فَيَنْبَنِي عَلَى عِلْمٍ وَعَمَلٍ وَحَالٍ. فَالعِلْمُ هُوَ المِفْتَاحُ، وَالعَمَلُ هُوَ البِنَاءُ، وَالحَالُ هُوَ الثَّمَرَةُ المَرْجُوَّةُ الَّتِي تُشْرِقُ فِي نُورِ القَلْبِ. فَبِالعِلْمِ يَنْكَشِفُ فَسَادُ الهَوَى، وَبِالعَمَلِ تَنْكَسِرُ حِدَّةُ الشَّهْوَةِ، وَبِالحَالِ يَسْتَقِرُّ الأُنْسُ بِاللَّهِ وَالرِّضَا بِقَضَائِهِ.

#### Master AQL Translation:
> As for the therapeutic course and the attainment of this virtue, it is anchored upon three reciprocal pillars: Knowledge (*ʿIlm*), Action (*ʿAmal*), and Spiritual State (*Ḥāl*). Knowledge is the opening key, action is the foundational architecture, and the spiritual state is the cherished fruit that radiates within the luminescence of the purified heart. Through knowledge, the corruption of caprice is exposed; through action, the severity of egoic desire is broken; and through the spiritual state, intimate serenity with Allāh and sublime contentment with His pre-eternal decree are irrevocably established.
