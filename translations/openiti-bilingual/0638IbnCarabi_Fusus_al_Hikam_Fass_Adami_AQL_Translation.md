## ۞ فَصُّ حِكْمَةٍ إِلَهِيَّةٍ فِي كَلِمَةٍ آدَمِيَّةٍ — مِرْآةُ الْوُجُودِ ۞
### The Ringstone of Divine Wisdom in the Word of Adam: The Mirror of Being
> *The cosmological necessity of the creation of Adam as the conscious reflective mirror of the Divine Reality.*

✦ ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━ ✦

#### ✦ المقطع [2.1] ✦

> ### لَمَّا شَاءَ الْحَقُّ سُبْحَانَهُ أَنْ يَرَى أَعْيَانَ أَسْمَائِهِ الْحُسْنَى الَّتِي لَا تَنْحَصِرُ، أَوْ قُلْتَ أَنْ يَرَى عَيْنَهُ فِي كَوْنٍ جَامِعٍ يَحْصُرُ الأَمْرَ كُلَّهُ لِكَوْنِهِ مُتَّصِفًا بِالْوُجُودِ، وَيَظْهَرُ بِهِ سِرُّهُ إِلَيْهِ.
> 
> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"When the Divine Reality, Transcendent and Exalted, willed to perceive the essential entities of His unconstrained Most Beautiful Names—or say, to behold His own Essence in a comprehensive cosmos that encompasses the entirety of the Affair by virtue of being qualified with existence, and wherein His inner secret is made manifest unto Him."*

✦ ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────── ✦

#### ✦ المقطع [2.2] ✦

> ### فَإِنَّ رُؤْيَةَ الشَّيْءِ نَفْسَهُ بِنَفْسِهِ لَيْسَتْ مِثْلَ رُؤْيَتِهِ نَفْسَهُ فِي أَمْرٍ آخَرَ يَكُونُ لَهُ كَالْمِرْآةِ؛ فَإِنَّهُ يَظْهَرُ لَهُ نَفْسُهُ فِي صُورَةٍ يُعْطِيهَا الْمَحَلُّ الْمَنْظُورُ فِيهِ.
> 
> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"For indeed, an entity's viewing of itself through itself is not identical to its beholding itself within another medium that serves as a reflective mirror; for in the mirror, itself appears to it in the configuration imparted by the very locus through which it is beheld."*

✦ ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────── ✦

#### ✦ المقطع [2.3] ✦

> ### وَقَدْ كَانَ الْحَقُّ سُبْحَانَهُ أَوْجَدَ الْعَالَمَ كُلَّهُ وُجُودَ شَبَحٍ مُسَوًّى لَا رُوحَ فِيهِ، فَكَانَ كَمِرْآةٍ غَيْرِ مَجْلُوَّةٍ؛ فَقْتَضَى الأَمْرُ جَلَاءَ مِرْآةِ الْعَالَمِ، فَكَانَ آدَمُ عَيْنَ جَلَاءِ تِلْكَ الْمِرْآةِ وَرُوحَ تِلْكَ الصُّورَةِ.
> 
> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"And the Divine Reality Exalted had already brought the entirety of the cosmos into existence as a fashioned bodily phantom devoid of a spirit—like unto an unpolished mirror; thus the ontological Affair necessitated the polishing of the mirror of the cosmos, and Adam was the very luster of that mirror and the living spirit of that form!"*

✦ ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────── ✦


## ۞ الإِنْسَانُ الْكَامِلُ: إِنْسَانُ الْعَيْنِ وَخَاتَمُ الْخَزَائِنِ ۞
### The Perfect Human: The Pupil of the Eye and the Seal of the Cosmic Treasury
> *Adam as the metaphysical nexus uniting celestial and terrestrial planes.*

✦ ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━ ✦

#### ✦ المقطع [3.1] ✦

> ### فَسُمِّيَ هَذَا الْمَذْكُورُ إِنْسَانًا وَخَلِيفَةً؛ فَأَمَّا إِنْسَانِيَّتُهُ فَلِعُمُومِ نَشْأَتِهِ وَحَصْرِهِ الْحَقَائِقَ كُلَّهَا، وَهُوَ لِلْحَقِّ بِمَنْزِلَةِ إِنْسَانِ الْعَيْنِ مِنَ الْعَيْنِ الَّذِي يَكُونُ بِهِ النَّظَرُ، وَهُوَ الْمُعَبَّرُ عَنْهُ بِالْبَصَرِ.
> 
> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"Thus was this created entity designated as 'Human' (Insān) and 'Vicegerent' (Khalīfah); as for his humanity, it arises from the universality of his constitution and his encompassing of all cosmic realities; and he stands in relation to the Divine Reality as the pupil of the eye (insān al-ʿayn) stands to the physical eye, whereby vision is actualized, which is designated as perception."*

✦ ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────── ✦

#### ✦ المقطع [3.2] ✦

> ### فَلِهَذَا سُمِّيَ إِنْسَانًا، فَإِنَّهُ بِهِ يَنْظُرُ الْحَقُّ إِلَى خَلْقِهِ فَيَرْحَمُهُمْ؛ فَهُوَ الإِنْسَانُ الْحَادِثُ الأَزَلِيُّ، وَالنَّشْءُ الدَّائِمُ الأَبَدِيُّ، وَالْكَلِمَةُ الْفَاصِلَةُ الْجَامِعَةُ.
> 
> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"For this reason was he termed Insān, because through him the Divine Reality gazes upon His creation and extends mercy unto them; he is the contingent-temporal yet primordial human, the perpetual and everlasting emergence, and the definitive, synthesizing Word."*

✦ ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────── ✦

#### ✦ المقطع [3.3] ✦

> ### فَهُوَ مِنَ الْعَالَمِ كَفَصِّ الْخَاتَمِ مِنَ الْخَاتَمِ، وَهُوَ مَحَلُّ النَّقْشِ وَالْعَلَامَةِ الَّتِي بِهَا يَخْتِمُ الْمَلِكُ عَلَى خَزَانَتِهِ؛ وَسَمَّاهُ خَلِيفَةً مِنْ أَجْلِ هَذَا، لِأَنَّهُ تَعَالَى الْحَافِظُ بِهِ خَلْقَهُ كَمَا يَحْفَظُ الْخَتْمُ الْخَزَائِنَ.
> 
> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"Thus in relation to the cosmos, he is like the gemstone ringstone (Faṣṣ) unto the ring: the locus of the engraved seal and the sovereign hallmark whereby the King seals his royal treasury; and He termed him Vicegerent (Khalīfah) for this precise cause, because Allāh Exalted preserves His creation through him, just as the imperial seal preserves royal treasuries."*

✦ ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────── ✦

#### ✦ المقطع [3.4] ✦

> ### فَلَا يَزَالُ الْعَالَمُ مَحْفُوظًا مَا دَامَ فِيهِ هَذَا الإِنْسَانُ الْكَامِلُ؛ أَلَا تَرَاهُ إِذَا زَالَ وَفُكَّ مِنْ خَزَانَةِ الدُّنْيَا لَمْ يَبْقَ فِيهَا مَا اخْتَزَنَهُ الْحَقُّ فِيهَا، وَخَرَجَ مَا كَانَ فِيهَا، وَانْتَقَلَ الأَمْرُ إِلَى الآخِرَةِ؟!
> 
> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"The cosmos continues to be preserved in equilibrium so long as this Perfect Human abides within it; dost thou not observe that when he departs and the seal is broken from the treasury of this earthly realm, naught of what the Divine Reality had stored within it remains, its hidden contents emerge, and the cosmic Affair is translated unto the Hereafter?!"*
