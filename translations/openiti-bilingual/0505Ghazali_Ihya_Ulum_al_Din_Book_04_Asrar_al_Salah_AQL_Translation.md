## 📜 The Sacred Opening (بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ)

**In the Name of Allāh, the Infinitely Boundless in Originating Mercy, the Continuously Dispensing of Sustaining Grace.**

---

### Prologue: The Divine Invitation to Intimate Discourse (المُنَاجَاةُ الإِلَهِيَّةُ)

#### النَّصُّ الْعَرَبِيُّ الأَصِيلُ:
> الحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي غَمَرَ العِبَادَ بِلَطَائِفِهِ، وَعَمَّرَ قُلُوبَهُمْ بِأَنْوَارِ الدِّينِ وَوَظَائِفِهِ، الَّتِي تَنْزِلُ عَنْ عَرْشِ الجَلَالِ إِلَى السَّمَاءِ الدُّنْيَا مِنْ دَرَجَاتِ الرَّحْمَةِ إِحْدَى عَوَاطِفِهِ. فَارَقَ المُلُوكَ مَعَ التَّفَرُّدِ بِالجَلَالِ وَالكِبْرِيَاءِ بِتَرْغِيبِ الخَلْقِ فِي السُّؤَالِ وَالدُّعَاءِ، فَقَالَ: «هَلْ مِنْ دَاعٍ فَأَسْتَجِيبَ لَهُ؟ وَهَلْ مِنْ مُسْتَغْفِرٍ فَأَغْفِرَ لَهُ؟»  
>  
> وَبَايَنَ السَّلَاطِينَ بِفَتْحِ البَابِ وَرَفْعِ الحِجَابِ، فَرَخَّصَ لِلْعِبَادِ فِي المُنَاجَاةِ بِالصَّلَوَاتِ كَيْفَمَا تَقَلَّبَتْ بِهِمُ الحَالَاتُ، فِي الجَمَاعَاتِ وَالخَلَوَاتِ، وَلَمْ يَقْتَصِرْ عَلَى الرُّخْصَةِ بَلْ تَلَطَّفَ بِالتَّرْغِيبِ وَالدَّعْوَةِ، وَغَيْرُهُ مِنْ ضُعَفَاءِ المُلُوكِ لَا يَسْمَحُ بِالخَلْوَةِ إِلَّا بَعْدَ تَقْدِيمِ الهَدِيَّةِ وَالرِّشْوَةِ! فَسُبْحَانَهُ مَا أَعْظَمَ شَأْنَهُ، وَأَقْوَى سُلْطَانَهُ، وَأَتَمَّ لُطْفَهُ، وَأَعَمَّ إِحْسَانَهُ!  
>  
> أَمَّا بَعْدُ، فَإِنَّ الصَّلَاةَ عِمَادُ الدِّينِ، وَعِصَامُ اليَقِينِ، وَرَأْسُ القُرُبَاتِ، وَغُرَّةُ الطَّاعَاتِ. وَنَحْنُ الآنَ نَقْتَصِرُ عَلَى مَا لَا بُدَّ لِلْمُرِيدِ مِنْهُ مِنْ أَعْمَالِهَا الظَّاهِرَةِ وَأَسْرَارِهَا البَاطِنَةِ، وَكَاشِفُونَ عَنْ دَقَائِقِ مَعَانِيهَا الخَفِيَّةِ فِي مَعَانِي الخُشُوعِ وَالإِخْلَاصِ وَالنِّيَّةِ.

#### Master AQL Translation:
> All universal praise belongs intrinsically to Allāh, who submerged His servants within His subtle graces, and illuminated their hearts with the radiances of faith and its sacred devotions, descending from the Throne of Majesty unto the lowest heaven as an outpouring of His mercy.  
>  
> He differed from worldly monarchs—despite His absolute singularity in majesty and transcendent pride—by entreating created beings to petition and beseech Him, proclaiming: *"Is there any suppliant that I may answer him? Is there any seeker of forgiveness that I may forgive him?"*  
>  
> And He stood apart from earthly rulers by unbarring the gate and lifting the veil, granting leave unto His servants to enter into intimate discourse (*Al-Munājāh*) through the sacred prayers in every fluctuating state of their existence, whether in congregations or in solitary retreat. Nor did He restrict Himself to mere permission; rather, He drew near through gentle enticement and divine summons—whereas frail worldly kings permit no private audience save following the presentation of gifts and tribute! Glory be to Him! How magnificent is His sovereign affair, how invincible His dominion, how consummate His loving-kindness, and how all-encompassing His beneficence!  
>  
> To proceed: Verily, prayer is the pillar of religion, the fortress of certainty, the foremost of all devotions, and the crown of acts of obedience. In this book, we confine ourselves unto what is indispensable for the spiritual seeker regarding both its outward physical actions and its inward mysteries, unveiling its subtle spiritual secrets regarding presence of heart (*Khushūʿ*), absolute sincerity (*Ikhlāṣ*), and intention (*Niyyah*).

### Section II: The Six Inward Conditions of the Soul in Prayer (الأَحْوَالُ البَاطِنَةُ فِي الصَّلَاةِ)

#### النَّصُّ الْعَرَبِيُّ الأَصِيلُ:
> اعْلَمْ أَنَّ الأَوْصَافَ البَاطِنَةَ الَّتِي تَتِمُّ بِهَا حَيَاةُ الصَّلَاةِ سِتَّةُ أُمُورٍ: حُضُورُ القَلْبِ، وَالتَّفَهُّمُ، وَالتَّعْظِيمُ، وَالهَيْبَةُ، وَالرَّجَاءُ، وَالحَيَاءُ.  
>  
> **فَالأَوَّلُ: حُضُورُ القَلْبِ**: وَنَعْنِي بِهِ أَنْ يَفْرُغَ القَلْبُ عَنْ غَيْرِ مَا هُوَ فِيهِ، فَيَكُونَ فِعْلُهُ مُقْتَرِنًا بِعِلْمِهِ، وَلَا يَكُونَ جَائِلًا فِي غَيْرِهِ.  
> **وَالثَّانِي: التَّفَهُّمُ**: لِمَعْنَى الكَلَامِ، وَهُوَ أَمْرٌ وَرَاءَ حُضُورِ القَلْبِ، فَرُبَّ حَاضِرِ القَلْبِ لَا يَفْهَمُ مَعْنَى اللَّفْظِ.  
> **وَالثَّالِثُ: التَّعْظِيمُ**: وَهُوَ حَالَةٌ لِلْقَلْبِ تَتَوَلَّدُ مِنْ مَعْرِفَتَيْنِ: مَعْرِفَةِ جَلَالِ اللَّهِ وَعَظَمَتِهِ، وَمَعْرِفَةِ حَقَارَةِ النَّفْسِ وَخِسَّتِهَا وَكَوْنِهَا عَبْدًا مَقْهُورًا.  
> **وَالرَّابِعُ: الهَيْبَةُ**: وَهِيَ خَوْفٌ مَصْدَرُهُ التَّعْظِيمُ، لِأَنَّ مَنْ خَافَ مِمَّنْ لَا يُعَظِّمُهُ لَا يُسَمَّى خَوْفُهُ هَيْبَةً.  
> **وَالخَامِسُ: الرَّجَاءُ**: فَرُبَّ مُعَظِّمٍ مَهَابٍ لَا يُرْجَى كَرَمُهُ، وَالعَبْدُ يَنْبَغِي أَنْ يَكُونَ رَاجِيًا بِصَلَاتِهِ ثَوَابَ اللَّهِ تَعَالَى كَمَا يَخَافُ عِقَابَهُ.  
> **وَالسَّادِسُ: الحَيَاءُ**: وَمَبْنَاهُ عَلَى اسْتِشْعَارِ التَّقْصِيرِ، وَعِلْمِهِ بِالعَجْزِ عَنِ القِيَامِ بِوَاجِبِ حَقِّ اللَّهِ تَعَالَى.

#### Master AQL Translation:
> Know that the inward spiritual conditions through which the vitality of prayer is realized are sixfold:  
>  
> 1. **Presence of the Heart (*Ḥuḍūr al-Qalb*)**: By which we mean that the heart empties itself of everything other than what it is engaged in, so that its physical action is intimately conjoined with its conscious awareness, wandering toward nothing else.  
> 2. **Comprehension (*Al-Tafahhum*)**: Grasping the inner meanings of the recited words; this is an advanced stage beyond mere presence, for a worshiper may be mentally attentive yet unmindful of the deep semantic realities of the speech.  
> 3. **Veneration (*Al-Taʿẓīm*)**: A state of the heart generated from two simultaneous recognitions: the recognition of the transcendent Majesty and Grandeur of Allāh, and the recognition of the humility, fragility, and servitude of the created ego.  
> 4. **Awe (*Al-Haybah*)**: A reverent fear whose origin is veneration; for one who fears an entity whom he does not venerate experiences ordinary dread, not sacred awe.  
> 5. **Hope (*Al-Rajā'*)**: For a worshiper may venerate a master yet despair of his generosity; but the servant must anticipate through his prayer the boundless grace of Allāh while revering His justice.  
> 6. **Spiritual Modesty (*Al-Ḥayā'*)**: Grounded in the deep perception of personal deficiency and the confession of incapacity to fulfill the absolute right of the Sovereign King.
