### (2) [Narrative: Regarding His Statement, Exalted is He, When You Stand Up for Prayer, Wash Your Faces and Arms Up to the Elbows]

#1 Narrated by Qutaybah ibn Sa’eed who said: “I was related to Sufyan who narrated from al-Zahri who reported from Abu Salama who reported from Abu Huraira that the Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa Sallam said: "When one of you awakens from his sleep, he should not immerse his hand in his ablution until he has washed it three times. ### (2) [Gate: The Habit of Using the *Sawak* Upon Rising From Night Prayer]

#2 Narrated by Ishaq ibn Ibrahim and Qutaybah ibn Saeed who reported from Jarir who related from Mansur who reported from Abu Wail about ‘Uthman bin Affan that the Messenger of Allāh ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa Sallam would, when he awoke at night, wipe his mouth with *sawak* (a tooth-cleaning twig).

### (3) [Gate: How One Should Cleanse Oneself]

#3 Narrated by Ahmad ibn ‘Abdah who said: “I was related to Hammad bin Zayd who reported from Ghilan ibn Jarir who related from Abu Burdah about Abu Musa that he entered upon the Messenger of Allāh ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa Sallam while he was chewing and rubbing *sawak* on his tongue, saying: “A’a, a’a.”
