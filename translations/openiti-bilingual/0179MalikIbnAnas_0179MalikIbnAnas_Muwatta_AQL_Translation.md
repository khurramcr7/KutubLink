**Translation of Malik Ibn Anas, *Al-Muwatta*, Book 1, Chapter: “The Time of Prayer”**

### I. Regarding the Timing of Prayer

**1.** Narrated by Muhammad ibn al-Hasan, who reported from Malik ibn Anas, that he related to يزيد بن زياد, a freedman of the Banu Hashim tribe, from عبد الله بن رافع, a freedman of Um Salama (may Allah be pleased with her), wife of the Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa Sallam, who said to Abu Huraira: “I will inform you. Pray Dhuhr [noon prayer] when your shadow is like yours, and ‘Asr [late afternoon prayer] when your shadow is like that of one whose shadow is shorter, and Maghrib [sunset prayer] when the sun sets, and Isha’ [night prayer] between you and a third of the night. If you sleep until half the night, then your eyes do not close, and pray Fajr [dawn prayer] with silence.”

**2.** Narrated by Malik: He related to us from Ibn Sha'hab al-Zahri, who reported from ‘Uroba, that he said to him: “I heard it from Aishah (may Allah be pleased with her), that the Messenger of Allāh ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa Sallam would pray ‘Asr and Dhuhr when the sun was in his chamber before it appeared.”

**3.** Narrated by Malik: He related to us from Ibn Sha'hab al-Zahri, who reported from Anas ibn Mālik, that he said: “We used to pray ‘Asr, then a person would go to Qiba’ [a location outside Medina], and they would find them praying ‘Asr.”

**4.** Narrated by Malik: He related to us from Isḥāq ibn ʿAbd Allāh ibn Abi Talha, who reported from Anas ibn Mālik, that he said: “We used to pray ‘Asr, then a person would go out to the Bani Amru bin ‘Awf tribe, and they would find them praying ‘Asr.’”

[Source: *Al-Muwatta*, 32/33]

**Note from the Translator:** Muhammad states: "This is the opinion of Abu Hanifa رحمه الله regarding the time of ‘Asr [late afternoon prayer], and he held that the 'Isfar' (increased sunlight) occurred in Fajar [dawn prayer]. As for our statement, we say: When the shadow exceeds its equivalent, becoming like a thing, and when there is an increase from the moment the sun sets, then the time of ‘Asr has entered.”
