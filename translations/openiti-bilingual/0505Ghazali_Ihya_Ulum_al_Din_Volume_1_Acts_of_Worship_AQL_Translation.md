## ۞ بَيَانُ الدَّاءِ الْعَامِّ وَغَفْلَةِ الْعَصْرِ ۞

> 
> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"For indeed, what has untied the knot of silence from my tongue and placed upon me the covenant of speech and the collar of proclamation is your persistent blindness to the clear reality of Truth, coupled with your stubborn persistence in aiding falsehood and embellishing ignorance."*

#### ✦ المقطع [2.2] ✦

> ### وَلَعَمْرِي إِنَّهُ لَا سَبَبَ لِإِصْرَارِكَ عَلَى التَّكَبُّرِ إِلَّا الدَّاءُ الَّذِي عَمَّ الْجَمَّ الْغَفِيرَ بَلْ شَمِلَ الْجَمَاهِيرَ، مِنَ الْقُصُورِ عَنْ مُلَاحَظَةِ ذِرْوَةِ هَذَا الأَمْرِ.
> 
> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"By my life, there is no cause for your persistent arrogance save the chronic disease that has infected the vast multitude—nay, engulfed the masses—of falling short in perceiving the paramount gravity of this existential reality!"*

✦ ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────── ✦

#### ✦ المقطع [2.3] ✦

> ### وَالْجَهْلِ بِأَنَّ الأَمْرَ إِدٌّ، وَالْخَطْبَ جِدٌّ، وَالآخِرَةَ مُقْبِلَةٌ، وَالدُّنْيَا مُدْبِرَةٌ، وَالأَجَلَ قَرِيبٌ، وَالسَّفَرَ بَعِيدٌ، وَالزَّادَ طَفِيفٌ، وَالْخَطَرَ عَظِيمٌ، وَالطَّرِيقَ سُدٌّ!
> 
> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"And their utter ignorance that the Affair is grave, the matter momentous, the Hereafter approaching, this worldly realm in retreat, the appointed hour near, the journey far, the provisions meager, the peril immense, and the path arduous!"*

✦ ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────── ✦

#### ✦ المقطع [2.4] ✦

> ### وَمَا سِوَى الْخَالِصِ لِوَجْهِ اللَّهِ مِنَ الْعِلْمِ وَالْعَمَلِ عِنْدَ النَّاقِدِ الْبَصِيرِ رَدٌّ!
> 
> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"And that everything short of that which is purely sincere for the Noble Face of Allāh—in both sacred knowledge and righteous action—is, in the sight of the All-Discerning Critic, irrevocably rejected!"*

> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ proclaimed: 'Seeking knowledge is an absolute sacred obligation incumbent upon every single Muslim.'"*

✦ ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────── ✦

#### ✦ المقطع [3.2] ✦

> ### وَقَالَ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ: «مَنْ سَلَكَ طَرِيقًا يَلْتَمِسُ فِيهِ عِلْمًا سَهَّلَ اللَّهُ لَهُ بِهِ طَرِيقًا إِلَى الْجَنَّةِ، وَإِنَّ الْمَلَائِكَةَ لَتَضَعُ أَجْنِحَتَهَا رِضًا لِطَالِبِ الْعِلْمِ».
> 
> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"And he ﷺ said: 'Whosoever traverses a path seeking therein sacred knowledge, Allāh makes easy for him thereby a path to Paradise; and verily the Angels lower their wings in blissful pleasure for the seeker of knowledge.'"*

✦ ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────── ✦

#### ✦ المقطع [3.3] ✦

> ### وَاعْلَمْ أَنَّ الْفَرْضَ الْعَيْنِيَّ مِنَ الْعِلْمِ هُوَ عِلْمُ كَيْفِيَّةِ الْعَمَلِ الْوَاجِبِ؛ فَمَنْ بَلَغَ وَجَبَ عَلَيْهِ مَعْرِفَةُ التَّوْحِيدِ وَمَا يَلْزَمُهُ فِي حِينِهِ مِنْ طَهَارَةٍ وَصَلَاةٍ، وَإِذَا دَخَلَ رَمَضَانُ لَزِمَهُ عِلْمُ الصَّوْمِ.
> 
> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"Know with certainty that the individual obligation (Farḍ al-ʿAyn) of knowledge is the knowledge of how to perform whatever duty is immediately incumbent upon the soul: thus, upon reaching maturity, one must know Divine Unity and the immediate requisites of purification and prayer; and when Ramadan enters, the knowledge of fasting becomes an obligatory science."*

✦ ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────── ✦

#### ✦ المقطع [3.4] ✦

> ### وَالْعِلْمُ يَنْقَسِمُ إِلَى عِلْمِ مُعَامَلَةٍ وَعِلْمِ مُكَاشَفَةٍ؛ فَعِلْمُ الْمُكَاشَفَةِ هُوَ غَايَةُ الْمَطْلُوبِ، وَهُوَ نُورٌ يَقْذِفُهُ اللَّهُ فِي الْقَلْبِ بَعْدَ تَطْهِيرِهِ مِنَ الصِّفَاتِ الْمَذْمُومَةِ.
> 
> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"Knowledge divides into the Science of Practical Conduct (ʿIlm al-Muʿāmalah) and the Science of Direct Unveiling (ʿIlm al-Mukāshafah); the science of unveiling is the ultimate summit of all spiritual seeking, being a divine light cast by Allāh into the purified heart once it is emptied of blameworthy characteristics."*

✦ ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────── ✦

#### ✦ المقطع [7.2] ✦

> ### فَأَمَّا صَوْمُ الْعُمُومِ فَهُوَ كَفُّ الْبَطْنِ وَالْفَرْجِ عَنْ قَضَاءِ الشَّهْوَةِ؛ وَأَمَّا صَوْمُ الْخُصُوصِ فَهُوَ كَفُّ السَّمْعِ وَالْبَصَرِ وَاللِّسَانِ وَالْيَدِ وَالرِّجْلِ وَسَائِرِ الْجَوَارِحِ عَنِ الآثَامِ.
> 
> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"As for the Fast of the General Multitude, it is merely refraining the stomach and private parts from fulfilling physical desire; whereas the Fast of the Spiritual Elect is restraining the hearing, sight, tongue, hands, feet, and all bodily faculties from every sinful blemish."*

#### ✦ المقطع [7.3] ✦

> ### وَأَمَّا صَوْمُ خُصُوصِ الْخُصُوصِ فَهُوَ صَوْمُ الْقَلْبِ عَنِ الْهِمَمِ الدَّنِيَّةِ وَالأَفْكَارِ الدُّنْيَوِيَّةِ، وَكَفُّهُ عَمَّا سِوَى اللَّهِ تَعَالَى بِالْكُلِّيَّةِ.
> 
> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"And as for the Fast of the Elect of the Elect, it is the fasting of the heart from base earthly ambitions and worldly thoughts, and its total and absolute restraint from everything other than Allāh the Exalted."*
