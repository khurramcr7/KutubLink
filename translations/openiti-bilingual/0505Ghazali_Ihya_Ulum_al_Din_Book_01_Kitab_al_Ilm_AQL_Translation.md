# ۞ الْبَابُ الأَوَّلُ: فِي فَضْلِ الْعِلْمِ وَالتَّعْلِيمِ وَالتَّعَلُّمِ وَشَوَاهِدِهِ مِنَ النَّقْلِ وَالْعَقْلِ ۞
## Chapter I: On the Excellence of Knowledge, Teaching, and Learning, with Evidence from Revelation and Reason

✦ ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━ ✦

### ✦ فَضِيلَةُ الْعِلْمِ: شَوَاهِدُهَا مِنَ الْقُرْآنِ الْكَرِيمِ ✦
#### The Excellence of Knowledge: Proofs from the Holy Qur'an

> ### شَهِدَ ٱللَّهُ أَنَّهُۥ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ وَٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ وَأُوْلُواْ ٱلۡعِلۡمِ قَآئِمَۢا بِٱلۡقِسۡطِۚ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ
> 
> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"Allāh bears witness that there is no deity worthy of worship except Him, and so do the Angels and those possessed of Knowledge, upholding all reality in consummate justice: there is no deity except Him, the Almighty, the All-Wise. [3:18]"*  
> 
> **Scholarly Exegesis & Commentary**:  
> *Observe how Allāh Exalted commenced with Himself, followed secondly by His Angels, and ranked thirdly the People of Knowledge; and let this suffice as an eternal testimony of their transcendent honor, nobility, and preeminence.*

✦ ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────── ✦

> ### يَرۡفَعِ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ مِنكُمۡ وَٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡعِلۡمَ دَرَجَٰتٖۚ وَٱللَّهُ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ خَبِيرٞ
> 
> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"Allāh will raise in ranks those who have believed among you and those who were granted Knowledge; and Allāh is fully Aware of all that you do. [58:11]"*  
> 
> **Scholarly Exegesis & Commentary**:  
> *Ibn ʿAbbās (may Allāh be pleased with them both) declared: 'The Scholars possess ranks above ordinary believers by seven hundred degrees, the span between each degree being a journey of five hundred years.'*

✦ ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────── ✦

> ### قُلۡ هَلۡ يَسۡتَوِي ٱلَّذِينَ يَعۡلَمُونَ وَٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَۗ إِنَّمَا يَتَذَكَّرُ أُوْلُواْ ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ
> 
> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"Say: 'Are those who know equal to those who do not know?' Only the possessors of pure inner intellect take admonition! [39:9]"*  
> 
> **Scholarly Exegesis & Commentary**:  
> *A decisive rhetorical negation proving the ontological impossibility of equating enlightened discernment with uncultivated ignorance.*

✦ ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────── ✦

> ### إِنَّمَا يَخۡشَى ٱللَّهَ مِنۡ عِبَادِهِ ٱلۡعُلَمَٰٓؤُاْۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَزِيزٌ غَفُورٌ
> 
> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"It is only the Scholars among His servants who stand in true reverent awe of Allāh; verily Allāh is Almighty, Forgiving. [35:28]"*  
> 
> **Scholarly Exegesis & Commentary**:  
> *True sacred knowledge is defined not by discursive compilation, but by the depth of reverent awe and conscious God-fearing reality ignited within the heart.*

✦ ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────── ✦

### ✦ فَضِيلَةُ الْعِلْمِ: شَوَاهِدُهَا مِنَ السُّنَّةِ النَّبَوِيَّةِ ✦
#### The Excellence of Knowledge: Proofs from the Prophetic Sunnah

> ### قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ: «مَنْ يُرِدِ اللَّهُ بِهِ خَيْرًا يُفَقِّهْهُ فِي الدِّينِ».
> 
> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ said: 'Whosoever Allāh wills immense good for, He grants him deep comprehension (Fiqh) in the religion.'"*  
> 
> **Scholarly Exegesis & Commentary**:  
> *Fiqh here denotes not mere legalistic wrangling, but the penetrating spiritual discernment of religious realities and the secrets of the soul.*

✦ ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────── ✦

> ### وَقَالَ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ: «الْعُلَمَاءُ وَرَثَةُ الأَنْبِيَاءِ، وَإِنَّ الأَنْبِيَاءَ لَمْ يُوَرِّثُوا دِينَارًا وَلَا دِرْهَمًا، وَإِنَّمَا وَرَّثُوا الْعِلْمَ، فَمَنْ أَخَذَهُ أَخَذَ بِحَظٍّ وَافِرٍ».
> 
> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"And he ﷺ declared: 'The Scholars are the heirs of the Prophets; and verily the Prophets leave neither dinar nor dirham as inheritance, but leave only knowledge; whosoever grasps it has seized a boundless treasure.'"*  
> 
> **Scholarly Exegesis & Commentary**:  
> *There is no spiritual rank above Prophethood, and there is no honor above inheriting the living legacy of the Prophets.*

✦ ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────── ✦

> ### وَقَالَ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ: «يَسْتَغْفِرُ لِلْعَالِمِ كُلُّ شَيْءٍ حَتَّى الْحِيتَانُ فِي الْمَاءِ وَالطَّيْرُ فِي الْهَوَاءِ».
> 
> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"And he ﷺ said: 'All things seek forgiveness for the Scholar, even the fish within the water and the birds in the sky.'"*  
> 
> **Scholarly Exegesis & Commentary**:  
> *Because the righteous scholar teaches humanity benevolence toward all creation, all cosmic realms supplicate on their behalf.*

✦ ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────── ✦

### ✦ فَضِيلَةُ التَّعَلُّمِ وَالتَّعْلِيمِ ✦
#### The Excellence of Learning and Teaching

> ### قَالَ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ: «مَنْ سَلَكَ طَرِيقًا يَلْتَمِسُ فِيهِ عِلْمًا سَهَّلَ اللَّهُ لَهُ بِهِ طَرِيقًا إِلَى الْجَنَّةِ، وَإِنَّ الْمَلَائِكَةَ لَتَضَعُ أَجْنِحَتَهَا رِضًا لِطَالِبِ الْعِلْمِ».
> 
> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"The Prophet ﷺ proclaimed: 'Whosoever traverses a path seeking therein knowledge, Allāh makes smooth for him thereby a path to Paradise; and the Angels lower their wings in humble reverence for the seeker of knowledge.'"*  
> 
> **Scholarly Exegesis & Commentary**:  
> *The angelic lowering of wings symbolizes celestial escort, protective favor, and spiritual communion with the sincere student.*

✦ ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────── ✦

> ### وَقَالَ مُعَاذُ بْنُ جَبَلٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ: «تَعَلَّمُوا الْعِلْمَ، فَإِنَّ تَعَلُّمَهُ لِلَّهِ خَشْيَةٌ، وَطَلَبَهُ عِبَادَةٌ، وَمُذَاكَرَتَهُ تَسْبِيحٌ، وَالْبَحْثَ عَنْهُ جِهَادٌ، وَتَعْلِيمَهُ لِمَنْ لَا يَعْلَمُهُ صَدَقَةٌ، وَبَذْلَهُ لِأَهْلِهِ قُرْبَةٌ».
> 
> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"Muʿādh ibn Jabal (may Allāh be pleased with him) said: 'Acquire knowledge: for acquiring it for Allāh is reverent awe, seeking it is worship, discussing it is glorification, researching it is sacred struggle, teaching it to the unlettered is charity, and bestowing it upon its worthy seekers is proximity to the Divine.'"*  
> 
> **Scholarly Exegesis & Commentary**:  
> *A comprehensive encapsulation demonstrating that knowledge transmutes every cognitive action into a sublime act of devotional worship.*

# ۞ الْبَابُ الثَّانِي: فِي فَرْضِ الْعَيْنِ وَفَرْضِ الْكِفَايَةِ مِنْ عِلْمِ الدِّينِ، وَبَيَانِ حَدِّ الْفِقْهِ وَالْكَلاَمِ ۞
## Chapter II: On Individual and Communal Obligations in Sacred Sciences, and the Boundaries of Fiqh and Kalām

✦ ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━ ✦

### ✦ تَحْدِيدُ فَرْضِ الْعَيْنِ وَفَرْضِ الْكِفَايَةِ ✦
#### Delineating the Individual Duty (Farḍ al-ʿAyn) and Communal Duty (Farḍ al-Kifāyah)

> ### قَالَ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ: «طَلَبُ الْعِلْمِ فَرِيضَةٌ عَلَى كُلِّ مُسْلِمٍ». وَاخْتَلَفَ النَّاسُ فِي هَذَا الْفَرْضِ عَلَى عِشْرِينَ فِرْقَةً؛ وَالصَّحِيحُ أَنَّهُ عِلْمُ كَيْفِيَّةِ الْعَمَلِ الْوَاجِبِ عِنْدَ وُجُوبِهِ.
> 
> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"The Prophet ﷺ declared: 'Seeking knowledge is an obligatory duty upon every Muslim.' Scholars differed regarding which science constitutes this duty into twenty factions; yet the verified truth is that it is the knowledge of how to perform whatever religious duty is immediately incumbent upon the individual at its appointed hour."*  
> 
> **Scholarly Exegesis & Commentary**:  
> *If an individual reaches maturity, knowledge of the two testimonies of faith becomes obligatory; when the prayer time enters, purification and prayer become obligatory; if one possesses wealth, zakāt rules become obligatory.*

✦ ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────── ✦

> ### وَأَمَّا فَرْضُ الْكِفَايَةِ فَهُوَ كُلُّ عِلْمٍ لَا يُسْتَغْنَى عَنْهُ فِي قِوَامِ أُمُورِ الدُّنْيَا كَالطِّبِّ إِذْ هُوَ ضَرُورِيٌّ فِي حَاجَةِ بَقَاءِ الأَبْدَانِ، وَالْحِسَابِ فَإِنَّهُ ضَرُورِيٌّ فِي الْمُعَامَلَاتِ وَقِسْمَةِ الْوَصَايَا وَالْمَوَارِيثِ.
> 
> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"As for the Communal Obligation (Farḍ al-Kifāyah), it comprises every science indispensable for the upright sustenance of the affairs of this world: such as Medicine, which is necessary for the preservation of bodies, and Arithmetic, which is essential for commercial transactions and the distribution of estates and inheritances."*  
> 
> **Scholarly Exegesis & Commentary**:  
> *Al-Ghazālī establishes that secular sciences indispensable for societal well-being are religious communal imperatives whose abandonment incurs collective guilt upon the community.*

✦ ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────── ✦

### ✦ الْفَرْقُ بَيْنَ عِلْمِ الْمُعَامَلَةِ وَعِلْمِ الْمُكَاشَفَةِ ✦
#### The Distinction Between the Science of Conduct and the Science of Unveiling

> ### عِلْمُ الْمُكَاشَفَةِ هُوَ عِلْمُ الْبَاطِنِ، وَهُوَ غَايَةُ الْعُلُومِ؛ وَهُوَ نُورٌ يَلْمَعُ فِي الْقَلْبِ عِنْدَ تَطْهِيرِهِ مِنَ الصِّفَاتِ الْمَذْمُومَةِ، فَيَنْكَشِفُ بِهِ أُمُورٌ كَانَ يُسْمَعُ أَسْمَاؤُهَا فَيُدْرَكُ بِالْعِيَانِ حَقِيقَةُ الذَّاتِ وَالصِّفَاتِ وَالأَفْعَالِ.
> 
> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"The Science of Unveiling (ʿIlm al-Mukāshafah) is the science of the inner soul and the ultimate apex of all sciences; it is a transcendent light that scintillates within the heart upon its purification from blameworthy vices, through which realities previously known only by name become directly witnessed by inner sight: the realities of the Divine Essence, Attributes, and Actions."*  
> 
> **Scholarly Exegesis & Commentary**:  
> *This science cannot be committed to writing; it is acquired through spiritual discipline, purification of the heart, and following the Sunnah.*

## Chapter III: On What the Multitude Counts Among Religious Sciences but is Not, and the Nature of Blameworthy Knowledge

✦ ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━ ✦

### ✦ أَسْبَابُ ذَمِّ بَعْضِ الْعُلُومِ ✦
#### Why Certain Branches of Knowledge are Deemed Blameworthy

> ### إِنَّ الْعِلْمَ لَا يُذَمُّ لِعَيْنِهِ، وَإِنَّمَا يُذَمُّ فِي حَقِّ الْعِبَادِ لِأَحَدِ ثَلَاثَةِ أَسْبَابٍ: إِمَّا لِأَنَّهُ يُؤَدِّي إِلَى ضَرَرٍ كَالسِّحْرِ، أَوْ لِأَنَّهُ ضَارٌّ فِي غَالِبِ الأَمْرِ كَالتَّنْجِيمِ، أَوْ لِأَنَّ الْخَوْضَ فِيهِ لَا يُفِيدُ نَفْعًا بَلْ يَشْغَلُ عَمَّا هُوَ أَهَمُّ.
> 
> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"Knowledge is never blameworthy in its essential nature, but is deemed blameworthy with respect to human beings for one of three causes: either because it leads intrinsically to harm (such as Sorcery), or because it is harmful in the vast majority of cases (such as judicial Astrology), or because immersing oneself in it yields no beneficial fruit while distracting from what is paramount."*  
> 
> **Scholarly Exegesis & Commentary**:  
> *A masterwork of epistemic classification: truth itself is noble, but practical immersion is judged by spiritual and societal consequences.*

### ✦ تَبَدُّلُ مَعَانِي الأَلْفَاظِ الإِسْلَامِيَّةِ ✦
#### The Semantic Distortion of Foundational Islamic Terms

> ### قَدْ بُدِّلَتْ أَلْفَاظٌ كَثِيرَةٌ وَحُرِّفَتْ عَنْ مَعَانِيهَا الأُولَى: كَالْفِقْهِ الَّذِي كَانَ يُطْلَقُ عَلَى عِلْمِ طَرِيقِ الآخِرَةِ وَمَعْرِفَةِ دَقَائِقِ آفَاتِ النُّفُوسِ فَخُصِّصَ بِفُرُوعِ الطَّلَاقِ وَالْعَتَاقِ وَاللِّعَانِ!
> 
> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"Verily, many foundational terms have been altered and shifted from their primordial meanings: such as 'Fiqh', which in the first century was applied to the science of the path to the Hereafter and the subtle knowledge of the soul's afflictions, but has now been constricted exclusively to the judicial minutiae of divorce, emancipation, and oaths of imprecation!"*  
> 
> **Scholarly Exegesis & Commentary**:  
> *Al-Ghazālī tracks how terms like Fiqh (Deep Discernment), ʿIlm (Sacred Insight), Tawḥīd (Experiential Monotheism), and Ḥikmah (Wisdom) were historically degraded from living spiritual sciences into legalistic and dialectical instruments.*

## Chapter IV: On the Calamities of Polemical Disputation and the Causes of Obsession with Debate

### ✦ الآفَاتُ الْعَشْرُ لِلْمُنَاظَرَةِ التَّنَافُسِيَّةِ ✦
#### The Ten Spiritual Afflictions Generated by Competitive Disputation

> ### اِعْلَمْ أَنَّ الْمُنَاظَرَةَ الْمَقْصُودَ بِهَا الْغَلَبَةُ وَالإِفْحَامُ هِيَ مَنْبَعُ الأَخْلَاقِ الْمَذْمُومَةِ كُلِّهَا؛ وَمِنْ آفَاتِهَا: الْحَسَدُ، وَالْكِبْرُ، وَالْحِقْدُ، وَالْغِيبَةُ، وَتَزْكِيَةُ النَّفْسِ، وَالتَّجَسُّسُ عَلَى عَوَرَاتِ الْخُصُومِ، وَالْفَرَحُ بِمَسَاءَةِ النَّاسِ، وَالنِّفَاقُ.
> 
> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"Know with certainty that disputation conducted for the purpose of victory, silencing adversaries, and public display is the poisonous fountainhead of all blameworthy character; among its afflictions are: Envy, Arrogance, Malice, Backbiting, Self-Righteousness, Spying out opponents' flaws, Rejoicing at another's humiliation, and Hypocrisy."*  
> 
> **Scholarly Exegesis & Commentary**:  
> *A devastating ethical analysis showing that scholarly ego-disputation destroys the inner heart far more rapidly than overt physical sins.*

✦ ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━ ✦

### ✦ آدَابُ طَالِبِ الْعِلْمِ ✦
#### The Requisite Conduct of the Spiritual Seeker

> ### الأَدَبُ الأَوَّلُ: تَقْدِيمُ طَهَارَةِ النَّفْسِ عَنْ رَذَائِلِ الأَخْلَاقِ، فَإِنَّ الْعِلْمَ عِبَادَةُ الْقَلْبِ وَصَلَاةُ السِّرِّ؛ وَكَمَا لَا تَصِحُّ صَلَاةُ الْجَوَارِحِ إِلَّا بِطَهَارَةِ الْبَدَنِ، فَلَا تَصِحُّ عِبَادَةُ الْقَلْبِ إِلَّا بِطَهَارَتِهِ عَنْ نَجَاسَاتِ الأَخْلَاقِ.
> 
> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"The First Duty of the Student: Prioritizing the purification of the soul from character vices; for knowledge is the inward worship of the heart and the secret prayer of the innermost consciousness; and just as the bodily prayer is invalid without physical purification, so too the heart's worship through sacred knowledge is invalid without cleansing from the spiritual filth of vices."*  
> 
> **Scholarly Exegesis & Commentary**:  
> *Knowledge is a divine light; it cannot dwell in a heart cluttered with pride, envy, or worldly ambition.*

✦ ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────── ✦

> ### الأَدَبُ الثَّانِي: أَنْ يُقَلِّلَ عَلَائِقَهُ الدُّنْيَوِيَّةَ وَيَنْأَى عَنِ الشَّوَاغِلِ، فَإِنَّ الْفِكْرَةَ مَتَى تَوَزَّعَتْ قَصُرَتْ عَنْ إِدْرَاكِ الْحَقَائِقِ.
> 
> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"The Second Duty: Minimizing worldly entanglements and distancing oneself from distracting preoccupations; for whenever thought is dispersed across scattered concerns, it falls short of apprehending the deep realities of Truth."*  
> 
> **Scholarly Exegesis & Commentary**:  
> *'Allāh has not placed for any man two hearts within his breast.' Complete immersion is the prerequisite for spiritual insight.*

✦ ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────── ✦

### ✦ آدَابُ الْمُعَلِّمِ وَالْمُرْشِدِ ✦
#### The Paramount Duties of the Instructor and Guide

> ### الْوَظِيفَةُ الأُولَى: الشَّفَقَةُ عَلَى الْمُتَعَلِّمِينَ وَأَنْ يُجْرِيَهُمْ مَجْرَى بَنِيهِ، قَالَ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ: «إِنَّمَا أَنَا لَكُمْ مِثْلُ الْوَالِدِ لِوَلَدِهِ».
> 
> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"The First Obligation of the Teacher: Overflowing compassion toward the students, treating them as if they were his own beloved children; as the Messenger ﷺ declared: 'Verily, I am unto you like a father unto his child.'"*  
> 
> **Scholarly Exegesis & Commentary**:  
> *The teacher seeks to deliver the student's soul from the eternal fire, a duty far superior to physical fatherhood which merely brings forth the earthly body.*

✦ ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────── ✦

> ### الْوَظِيفَةُ الثَّانِيَةُ: أَنْ يَقْتَدِيَ بِصَاحِبِ الشَّرْعِ فَلَا يَطْلُبَ عَلَى إِفَادَةِ الْعِلْمِ أُجْرَةً، وَلَا يَقْصِدَ بِهِ جَزَاءً وَلَا شُكُورًا، بَلْ يُعَلِّمُ لِوَجْهِ اللَّهِ تَعَالَى طَلَبًا لِلْقُرْبَةِ.
> 
> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"The Second Obligation: Emulating the Lawgiver ﷺ by seeking neither material wages nor worldly praise for imparting sacred knowledge, but teaching purely for the Noble Face of Allāh in pursuit of spiritual proximity."*  
> 
> **Scholarly Exegesis & Commentary**:  
> *The true scholar perceives the student as doing him a favor by accepting the spiritual seeds of wisdom, rather than viewing the student as indebted.*

✦ ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────── ✦


# ۞ الْبَابُ السَّادِسُ: فِي آفَاتِ الْعِلْمِ وَبَيَانِ عَلَامَاتِ عُلَمَاءِ الآخِرَةِ وَعُلَمَاءِ السُّوءِ ۞
## Chapter VI: On the Calamities of Knowledge and the Distinguishing Signs of the Scholars of the Hereafter versus the Scholars of Falsehood

✦ ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━ ✦

### ✦ عَلَامَاتُ عُلَمَاءِ الآخِرَةِ ✦
#### The Twelve Distinguishing Hallmark Traits of the Scholars of the Hereafter

> ### مِنْ عَلَامَاتِ عَالِمِ الآخِرَةِ: أَنْ لَا يَطْلُبَ بِعِلْمِهِ الدُّنْيَا؛ فَقَدْ كَانَ أَقَلُّ دَرَجَاتِ الْعَالِمِ أَنْ يُدْرِكَ حَقَارَةَ الدُّنْيَا وَخِسَّتَهَا وَسُرْعَةَ انْقِضَائِهَا، وَعِظَمَ الآخِرَةِ وَدَوَامَهَا وَأَبَدِيَّةَ نَعِيمِهَا.
> 
> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"Among the hallmarks of the Scholar of the Hereafter: That he never seeks through his knowledge this temporal world; for the very lowest threshold of true knowledge is to perceive the insignificance, baseness, and rapid vanishing of this fleeting world, contrasted with the majesty, permanence, and eternity of the Hereafter."*  
> 
> **Scholarly Exegesis & Commentary**:  
> *How can one who trades eternal pearls for temporal dung be deemed a scholar?! The authentic scholar lives as an exile traveling toward eternity.*

✦ ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────── ✦

> ### وَمِنْهَا: أَنْ يَكُونَ قَوْلُهُ مُوَافِقًا لِفِعْلِهِ، بَلْ لَا يَأْمُرُ بِشَيْءٍ إِلَّا وَهُوَ أَوَّلُ الْعَامِلِينَ بِهِ، وَلَا يَنْهَى عَنْ شَيْءٍ إِلَّا وَهُوَ أَبْعَدُ النَّاسِ عَنْهُ.
> 
> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"And among them: That his speech corresponds impeccably to his actions; nay, he commands nothing save that he is the foremost in practicing it, and forbids nothing save that he is the most distant of mankind from committing it."*  
> 
> **Scholarly Exegesis & Commentary**:  
> *Action is the fruit of knowledge; knowledge without action is an indictment against the bearer on the Day of Resurrection.*

✦ ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────── ✦


# ۞ الْبَابُ السَّابِعُ: فِي الْعَقْلِ وَشَرَفِهِ وَحَقِيقَتِهِ وَأَقْسَامِهِ ۞
## Chapter VII: On the Intellect (Al-ʿAql), its Transcendent Dignity, Reality, and Divisions

✦ ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━ ✦

### ✦ شَرَفُ الْعَقْلِ وَمَنْزِلَتُهُ فِي الدِّينِ ✦
#### The Sublime Status of the Intellect in Relation to Divine Revelation

> ### الْعَقْلُ هُوَ أُسُّ الْعِلْمِ وَيَنْبُوعُهُ وَأَصْلُهُ؛ وَهُوَ النُّورُ الَّذِي يُدْرَكُ بِهِ صِدْقُ النُّبُوَّةِ، وَمَثَلُ الْعَقْلِ مَعَ الشَّرْعِ كَمَثَلِ الْبَصَرِ مَعَ الشَّمْسِ: فَالْعَقْلُ كَالْبَصَرِ السَّلِيمِ، وَالشَّرْعُ كَالشَّمْسِ الْمُنِيرَةِ، فَلَا غِنَى لِأَحَدِهِمَا عَنِ الآخَرِ.
> 
> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"The Intellect is the foundation of sacred knowledge, its living spring, and its root; it is the inner light through which the truth of Prophethood is apprehended. The relation of the Intellect to Divine Revelation is like that of healthy sight to the radiant sun: the Intellect is the sound visual faculty, and Revelation is the illuminating sun—neither can function without the other!"*  
> 
> **Scholarly Exegesis & Commentary**:  
> *Al-Ghazālī demolishes both anti-rational dogmatism and anti-revelatory rationalism: Revelation provides the cosmic light, while Intellect is the eye that perceives it.*

✦ ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────── ✦

> ### وَالْعَقْلُ يُطْلَقُ عَلَى أَرْبَعَةِ مَعَانٍ: الْغَرِيزَةُ الَّتِي بِهَا يَتَمَيَّزُ الإِنْسَانُ عَنِ الْبَهَائِمِ، وَالْعُلُومُ الضَّرُورِيَّةُ، وَالْعُلُومُ التَّجْرِيبِيَّةُ، وَقُوَّةُ النَّفْسِ فِي قَمْعِ الشَّهَوَاتِ وَإِدْرَاكِ عَوَاقِبِ الأُمُورِ.
> 
> **AQL Master Translation**:  
> *"The Intellect is predicated upon four ascending meanings: The innate instinct whereby humanity is distinguished from beasts, the immediate necessary axiomatic truths, the empirical experiential sciences, and the soul's governing power in subduing physical passions to perceive the eternal consequences of actions."*  
> 
> **Scholarly Exegesis & Commentary**:  
> *The summit of intellect is moral and spiritual proportion: true intellect is that which steers the mortal traveler safely to eternal beatitude.*
