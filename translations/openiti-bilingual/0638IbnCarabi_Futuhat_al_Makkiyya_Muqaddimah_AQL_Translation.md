## 📜 The Sacred Opening (بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ)

**In the Name of Allāh, the Infinitely Boundless in Originating Mercy, the Continuously Dispensing of Sustaining Grace. May the peace and blessings of Allāh be upon our Master Muḥammad, his purified family, and his companions.**

---

### Part I: The Primordial Doxology of Ontological Creation (تَوْحِيدُ الإِيجَادِ)

#### النَّصُّ الْعَرَبِيُّ الأَصِيلُ:
> الحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَوْجَدَ الأَشْيَاءَ عَنْ عَدَمٍ وَعَدَمِهِ، وَأَوْقَفَ وُجُودَهَا عَلَى تَوَجُّهِ كَلِمِهِ، لِنُحَقِّقَ بِذَلِكَ سِرَّ حُدُوثِهَا وَقِدَمِهَا مِنْ قِدَمِهِ، وَنَقِفَ عِنْدَ هَذَا التَّحْقِيقِ عَلَى مَا أَعْلَمَنَا بِهِ مِنْ صِدْقِ قِدَمِهِ. فَظَهَرَ سُبْحَانَهُ وَظَهَرَ وَأَظْهَرَ وَمَا بَطَنَ، وَلَكِنَّهُ بَطَنَ وَأَبْطَنَ، وَأَثْبَتَ لَهُ الاسْمُ الأَوَّلُ وُجُودَ عَيْنِ العَبْدِ وَقَدْ كَانَ ثَبَتَ، وَأَثْبَتَ لَهُ الاسْمُ الآخِرُ تَقْدِيرَ الفَنَاءِ وَالفَقْدِ وَقَدْ كَانَ قَبْلَ ذَلِكَ ثَبَتَ، فَلَوْلَا العَصْرُ وَالمُعَاصِرُ، وَالجَاهِلُ وَالخَابِرُ، مَا عَرَفَ أَحَدٌ مَعْنَى اسْمِهِ الأَوَّلِ وَالآخِرِ، وَلَا البَاطِنِ وَالظَّاهِرِ.

#### Master AQL Translation:
> All universal praise belongs intrinsically to Allāh, who brought forth all entities into manifestation out of non-existence and out of its very non-being; who suspended their actualized existence upon the creative orientation of His primordial Word (*Kun* — Be!), so that we may verify thereby the profound secret of their contingent temporality alongside His eternal pre-existence, and so that we may stand in this verification upon what He has taught us of the absolute veracity of His eternity.  
>  
> He became manifest—Glory be to Him!—and shone forth, and caused to appear, and never truly concealed Himself; yet He remained inward, and caused inwardness to conceal. His Name *Al-Awwal* (The First) established the latent reality of the servant, which was already established in divine knowledge; while His Name *Al-Ākhir* (The Last) established the decree of dissolution and passing, which had likewise been previously determined.  
>  
> Were it not for time and the contemporary observer, the ignorant seeker and the discerning knower, none would have comprehended the true reality of His Names *Al-Awwal* and *Al-Ākhir*, nor *Al-Bāṭin* (The Inward) and *Al-Ẓāhir* (The Outward).

---

### Part II: The Mystery of Servanthood and Lordship (سِرُّ الرُّبُوبِيَّةِ وَالعُبُودِيَّةِ)

#### النَّصُّ الْعَرَبِيُّ الأَصِيلُ:
> وَإِنْ كَانَتْ أَسْمَاؤُهُ الحُسْنَى، عَلَى هَذَا الطَّرِيقِ الأَسْنَى، وَلَكِنْ بَيْنَهَا تَبَايُنٌ فِي المَنَازِلِ، يَتَبَيَّنُ ذَلِكَ عِنْدَمَا تُتَّخَذُ وَسَائِلَ لِحُلُولِ النَّوَازِلِ، فَلَيْسَ عَبْدُ الحَلِيمِ هُوَ عَبْدَ الكَرِيمِ، وَلَيْسَ عَبْدُ الغَفُورِ هُوَ عَبْدَ الشَّكُورِ، فَكُلُّ عَبْدٍ لَهُ اسْمٌ هُوَ رَبُّهُ، وَهُوَ جِسْمُ ذَلِكَ الاسْمِ قَبْلَهُ.  
>  
> الرَّبُّ حَقٌّ وَالعَبْدُ حَقٌّ ••• يَا لَيْتَ شِعْرِي مَنْ الْمُكَلَّفُ  
> إِنْ قُلْتَ عَبْدٌ فَذَاكَ مَيْتٌ ••• أَوْ قُلْتَ رَبٌّ أَنَّى يُكَلَّفُ  

#### Master AQL Translation:
> Although His Most Beautiful Names proceed upon this most exalted highway, there is nonetheless an ontological variance among their stations. This variance becomes luminous when the Names are invoked as spiritual instruments for the descent of divine decrees: for the servant of *Al-Ḥalīm* (The Clement) is not identical to the servant of *Al-Karīm* (The Generous); nor is the servant of *Al-Ghafūr* (The All-Forgiving) identical to the servant of *Al-Shakūr* (The All-Appreciative). Every created servant is governed by a particular Divine Name that is his immediate Lord, while the servant serves as the locus of manifestation for that Name before Him.  
>  
> *The Lord is an absolute reality, and the servant is an absolute reality!*  
> *Would that I knew: who then is the one charged with sacred obligation?*  
> *If you declare: 'It is the servant,' he is intrinsically dead without Him!*  
> *Or if you declare: 'It is the Lord,' how can the Sovereign Lord be charged with obligation?!*

---

### Part III: The Heavenly Vision in the World of Archetypes (مَشْهَدُ المِنْبَرِ فِي عَالَمِ المِثَالِ)

#### النَّصُّ الْعَرَبِيُّ الأَصِيلُ:
> وَالصَّلَاةُ عَلَى سِرِّ العَالَمِ وَنُكْتَتِهِ، وَمَطْلَبِ العَالَمِ وَبُغْيَتِهِ، السَّيِّدِ الصَّادِقِ، المُدْلِجِ إِلَى رَبِّهِ الطَّارِقِ، المُخْتَرَقِ بِهِ السَّبْعُ الطَّرَائِقِ، لِيُرِيَهُ مَنْ أَسْرَى بِهِ مَا أَوْدَعَ مِنَ الآيَاتِ وَالحَقَائِقِ. الَّذِي شَاهَدْتُهُ عِنْدَ إِنْشَائِي هَذِهِ الخُطْبَةَ فِي عَالَمِ حَقَائِقِ المِثَالِ، فِي حَضْرَةِ الجَلَالِ، مُكَاشَفَةً قَلْبِيَّةً فِي حَضْرَةٍ غَيْبِيَّةٍ.  
>  
> وَلَمَّا شَهِدْتُهُ فِي ذَلِكَ العَالَمِ سَيِّدًا، مَعْصُومَ المَقَاصِدِ مَحْفُوظَ المَشَاهِدِ، مَنْصُورًا مُؤَيَّدًا، وَجَمِيعُ الرُّسُلِ بَيْنَ يَدَيْهِ مُصْطَفُّونَ، وَأُمَّتُهُ الَّتِي هِيَ خَيْرُ أُمَّةٍ عَلَيْهِ مُلْتَفُّونَ، وَمَلَائِكَةُ التَّسْخِيرِ مِنْ حَوْلِ عَرْشِ مَقَامِهِ حَافُّونَ.  
>  
> فَالْتَفَتَ السَّيِّدُ الأَعْلَى، وَالمَوْرِدُ العَذْبُ الأَحْلَى، وَالنُّورُ الأَكْشَفُ الأَجْلَى، فَرَآنِي وَرَاءَ الخَتْمِ، لِاشْتِرَاكٍ بَيْنِي وَبَيْنَهُ فِي الحُكْمِ، فَقَالَ لَهُ السَّيِّدُ: هَذَا عَدِيلُكَ، وَابْنُكَ وَخَلِيلُكَ، انْصِبْ لَهُ مِنْبَرَ الطَّرْفَاءِ بَيْنَ يَدَيَّ، ثُمَّ أَشَارَ إِلَيَّ: أَنْ قُمْ يَا مُحَمَّدُ عَلَيْهِ فَأَثْنِ عَلَى مَنْ أَرْسَلَنِي وَعَلَيَّ، فَإِنَّ فِيكَ شَعْرَةً مِنِّي، لَا صَبْرَ لَهَا عَنِّي!

#### Master AQL Translation:
> And divine blessings be upon the primordial Secret and Core of the cosmos, the ultimate quest and yearning of creation: the Truthful Master, who journeyed by night unto his Lord, piercing through the seven celestial pathways, so that He who caused him to journey might display unto him the supreme signs and realities deposited throughout creation.  
>  
> I beheld him during the composition of this prologue in the World of Archetypal Realities (*ʿĀlam al-Mithāl*), in the Divine Presence of Majesty (*Ḥaḍrat al-Jalāl*), through an unveiled inner vision of the heart within the unseen realm.  
>  
> And when I witnessed him in that realm as the undisputed Master—infallible in intent, preserved in spiritual witnessing, triumphantly aided—with all the Prophets and Messengers arrayed in rows before his presence, and his community surrounding him as the best of all nations, and the angels of celestial governance circling the throne of his exalted station:  
>  
> The Supreme Master—the sweetest pristine fountainhead and the most radiant illuminating light—turned his gaze and beheld me standing behind the Seal of Sainthood, owing to a spiritual partnership between myself and him in divine governance. The Prophet ﷺ said unto the Seal: *"This is your equal counterpart, your son, and your intimate friend! Erect for him the Tamarisk Wood Pulpit (*Minbar al-Ṭarfā'*) before my presence!"*  
>  
> Then the Prophet ﷺ gestured unto me: *"Arise, O Muḥammad, ascend upon the pulpit, and praise Him who sent me, and praise me! For verily, within you resides a single strand of hair from my being that cannot endure separation from me!"*

### Part IV: The Inscription of the Pure Muhammadan Station (المَقَامُ المُحَمَّدِيُّ الأَطْهَرُ)

#### النَّصُّ الْعَرَبِيُّ الأَصِيلُ:
> فَنَصَبَ الخَتْمُ المِنْبَرَ فِي ذَلِكَ المَشْهَدِ الأَخْطَرِ، وَعَلَى جَبْهَةِ المِنْبَرِ مَكْتُوبٌ بِالنُّورِ الأَزْهَرِ: هَذَا هُوَ المَقَامُ المُحَمَّدِيُّ الأَطْهَرُ، مَنْ رَقَى فِيهِ فَقَدْ وَرِثَهُ، وَأَرْسَلَهُ الحَقُّ حَافِظًا لِحُرْمَةِ الشَّرِيعَةِ وَبَعَثَهُ.  
>  
> فَشَكَرْتُ اللَّهَ عَزَّ وَجَلَّ وَصَعِدْتُ أَعْلَاهُ، وَحَصَلْتُ فِي مَوْضِعِ وُقُوفِهِ وَمُسْتَوَاهُ، وَبُسِطَ لِي عَلَى الدَّرَجَةِ الَّتِي أَنَا فِيهَا كُمُّ قَمِيصٍ أَبْيَضَ فَوَقَفْتُ عَلَيْهِ، حَتَّى لَا أُبَاشِرَ المَوْضِعَ الَّذِي بَاشَرَهُ بِقَدَمَيْهِ، تَنْزِيهًا لَهُ وَتَشْرِيفًا، وَتَنْبِيهًا لَنَا وَتَعْرِيفًا، أَنَّ المَقَامَ الَّذِي شَاهَدَهُ مِنْ رَبِّهِ، لَا يُشَاهِدُهُ الوَرَثَةُ إِلَّا مِنْ وَرَاءِ ثَوْبِهِ.  
>  
> فَلَمَّا وَقَفْتُ ذَلِكَ المَوْقِفَ الأَسْنَى، بَيْنَ يَدَيْ مَنْ كَانَ مِنْ رَبِّهِ فِي لَيْلَةِ إِسْرَائِهِ قَابَ قَوْسَيْنِ أَوْ أَدْنَى، قُمْتُ مُقَنَّعًا خَجِلًا، ثُمَّ أُيِّدْتُ بِرُوحِ القُدُسِ فَافْتَتَحْتُ مُرْتَجِلًا:  
>  
> يَا مُنْزِلَ الآيَاتِ وَالأَنْبَاءِ ••• أَنْزِلْ عَلَيَّ مَعَالِمَ الأَسْمَاءِ  
> حَتَّى أَكُونَ لِحَمْدِ ذَاتِكَ جَامِعًا ••• بِمَحَامِدِ السَّرَّاءِ وَالضَّرَّاءِ  
> وَيَكُونَ هَذَا السَّيِّدُ العَلَمُ الَّذِي ••• جَرَّدْتَهُ مِنْ دَوْرَةِ الخُلَفَاءِ  
> وَجَعَلْتَهُ الأَصْلَ الكَرِيمَ وَآدَمٌ ••• مَا بَيْنَ طِينَةِ خَلْقِهِ وَالمَاءِ  

#### Master AQL Translation:
> Thereupon the Seal erected the pulpit within that momentous witnessing, and upon the crown of the pulpit was inscribed in radiant white light:  
> *"This is the Purest Muhammadan Station (*Al-Maqām al-Muḥammadī al-Aṭhar*). Whosoever ascends upon it has inherited his legacy, and the Divine Truth has dispatched him as a guardian over the sanctity of the Sacred Law."*  
>  
> I gave thanks unto Allāh, Mighty and Majestic, and ascended to its summit, standing upon his very platform. And there was spread beneath me upon the step where I stood the sleeve of a luminous white robe, upon which I stood, so that my feet would not touch the exact station that his blessed feet had touched—out of reverence and honor for him, and as an illumination for us that the ultimate station he witnessed directly from his Lord cannot be witnessed by his spiritual heirs save from behind the veil of his garment!  
>  
> When I stood in that most exalted station, before the presence of him who was on the Night of Ascension within *'two bow-lengths or nearer'* (Qur'an 53:9), I stood veiled in reverent awe. Then, supported by the Holy Spirit, I opened in spontaneous verse:  
>  
> *O Sender down of revelation and celestial tidings!*  
> *Descend upon my heart the landmarks of the Divine Names!*  
> *That I may encompass the praise of Thy transcendent Essence,*  
> *Through all laudations in seasons of ease and hardship!*  
> *And that this towering master, the radiant beacon,*  
> *Whom Thou hast distinguished above the cycle of worldly successors,*  
> *Whom Thou didst make the noble primordial root,*  
> *While Adam was yet between water and clay!*
