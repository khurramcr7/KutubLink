### Prologue: The Four Concentric Tiers of Purity (مَرَاتِبُ الطَّهَارَةِ الأَرْبَعُ)

#### النَّصُّ الْعَرَبِيُّ الأَصِيلُ:
> الحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي تَلَطَّفَ بِعِبَادِهِ فَتَعَبَّدَهُمْ بِالنَّظَافَةِ، وَأَفَاضَ عَلَى قُلُوبِهِمْ تَزْكِيَةً لِسَرَائِرِهِمْ أَنْوَارَهُ وَأَلْطَافَهُ، وَأَعَدَّ لِظَوَاهِرِهِمْ تَطْهِيرًا لَهَا المَاءَ المَخْصُوصَ بِالرِّقَّةِ وَاللَّطَافَةِ. وَصَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَى النَّبِيِّ مُحَمَّدٍ المُسْتَغْرِقِ بِنُورِ الهُدَى أَطْرَافَ العَالَمِ وَأَكْنَافَهُ، وَعَلَى آلِهِ الطَّيِّبِينَ الطَّاهِرِينَ.  
>  
> أَمَّا بَعْدُ، فَقَدْ قَالَ النَّبِيُّ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ: «مِفْتَاحُ الصَّلَاةِ الطُّهُورُ»، وَقَالَ تَعَالَى: ﴿فِيهِ رِجَالٌ يُحِبُّونَ أَن يَتَطَهَّرُوا وَاللَّهُ يُحِبُّ الْمُطَّهِّرِينَ﴾، وَقَالَ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ: «الطُّهُورُ شَطْرُ الإِيمَانِ».  
>  
> فَتَفَطَّنَ ذَوُو البَصَائِرِ بِهَذِهِ الظَّوَاهِرِ أَنَّ أَهَمَّ الأُمُورِ تَطْهِيرُ السَّرَائِرِ، إِذْ يَبْعُدُ أَنْ يَكُونَ المُرَادُ بِقَوْلِهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ «الطُّهُورُ شَطْرُ الإِيمَانِ» عِمَارَةَ الظَّاهِرِ بِالتَّنْظِيفِ بِإِفَاضَةِ المَاءِ وَإِلْقَائِهِ، وَتَخْرِيبَ البَاطِنِ وَإِبْقَاءَهُ مَشْحُونًا بِالأَخْبَاثِ وَالأَقْذَارِ! هَيْهَاتَ هَيْهَاتَ! وَالطَّهَارَةُ لَهَا أَرْبَعُ مَرَاتِبَ:  
>  
> المَرْتَبَةُ الأُولَى: تَطْهِيرُ الظَّاهِرِ عَنِ الأَحْدَاثِ وَعَنِ الأَخْبَاثِ وَالفَضَلَاتِ.  
> المَرْتَبَةُ الثَّانِيَةُ: تَطْهِيرُ الجَوَارِحِ عَنِ الجَرَائِمِ وَالآثَامِ.  
> المَرْتَبَةُ الثَّالِثَةُ: تَطْهِيرُ القَلْبِ عَنِ الأَخْلَاقِ المَذْمُومَةِ وَالرَّذَائِلِ المَمْقُوتَةِ.  
> المَرْتَبَةُ الرَّابِعَةُ: تَطْهِيرُ السِّرِّ عَمَّا سِوَى اللَّهِ تَعَالَى، وَهِيَ طَهَارَةُ الأَنْبِيَاءِ صَلَوَاتُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَالصِّدِّيقِينَ.

#### Master AQL Translation:
> All universal praise belongs intrinsically to Allāh, who treated His servants with loving subtlety, instructing them to worship Him through purity; who poured over their hearts—as a purification for their inward secrets—His illuminating radiances and subtle graces; and who prepared for their outward bodies water distinguished by fluidity and softness. May divine blessings be upon the Prophet Muḥammad, whose guiding light encompassed all horizons of the cosmos, and upon his purified family.  
>  
> To proceed: The Prophet ﷺ declared: *"The key to prayer is purification,"* and Allāh the Exalted proclaimed: *“Within it are men who love to purify themselves, and Allāh loves those who purify themselves”* (Qur'an 9:108), and the Prophet ﷺ affirmed: *"Purification is half of faith."*  
>  
> Those endowed with spiritual discernment recognized through these outward indicators that the paramount priority is the purification of the inward conscience. For it is utterly inconceivable that the Prophet's words *"Purification is half of faith"* should merely intend polishing the outward vessel through pouring water while leaving the inward heart in ruins, congested with spiritual impurities and filth! Far from it, far from it! Purification possesses four concentric tiers:  
>  
> **The First Tier**: Cleansing the outward body from ritual impurities, physical filth, and bodily wastes.  
> **The Second Tier**: Cleansing the bodily limbs from moral transgressions and sinful actions.  
> **The Third Tier**: Cleansing the heart from blameworthy character traits and repugnant vices (envy, pride, malice, ostentation).  
> **The Fourth Tier**: Cleansing the inmost secret (*Al-Sirr*) from everything other than Allāh the Exalted—and this is the transcendent purity of the Prophets and the Champions of Truth!

---

### Section II: The Mathematical Logic of "Purification is Half of Faith" (سِرُّ كَوْنِ الطَّهَارَةِ شَطْرَ الإِيمَانِ)

#### النَّصُّ الْعَرَبِيُّ الأَصِيلُ:
> وَالطَّهَارَةُ فِي كُلِّ رُتْبَةٍ نِصْفُ العَمَلِ الَّذِي فِيهَا، فَإِنَّ الغَايَةَ القُصْوَى فِي عَمَلِ السِّرِّ أَنْ يَنْكَشِفَ لَهُ جَلَالُ اللَّهِ تَعَالَى وَعَظَمَتُهُ، وَلَنْ تَحُلَّ مَعْرِفَةُ اللَّهِ تَعَالَى بِالحَقِيقَةِ فِي السِّرِّ مَا لَمْ يَرْتَحِلْ مَا سِوَى اللَّهِ تَعَالَى عَنْهُ.  
>  
> وَلِذَلِكَ قَالَ اللَّهُ عَزَّ وَجَلَّ: ﴿قُلِ اللَّهُ ثُمَّ ذَرْهُمْ فِي خَوْضِهِمْ يَلْعَبُونَ﴾، لِأَنَّهُمَا لَا يَجْتَمِعَانِ فِي قَلْبٍ: ﴿مَّا جَعَلَ اللَّهُ لِرَجُلٍ مِّن قَلْبَيْنِ فِي جَوْفِهِ﴾. وَأَمَّا عَمَلُ القَلْبِ فَالغَايَةُ القُصْوَى عِمَارَتُهُ بِالأَخْلَاقِ المَحْمُودَةِ وَالعَقَائِدِ المَشْرُوعَةِ، وَلَنْ يَتَّصِفَ بِهَا مَا لَمْ يُنَظَّفْ عَنْ نَقَائِضِهَا مِنَ العَقَائِدِ الفَاسِدَةِ وَالرَّذَائِلِ المَمْقُوتَةِ، فَكَانَ تَطْهِيرُهُ أَحَدَ الشَّطْرَيْنِ، وَهُوَ الشَّطْرُ الأَوَّلُ الَّذِي هُوَ شَرْطٌ فِي الثَّانِي، فَكَانَ الطُّهُورُ شَطْرَ الإِيمَانِ بِهَذَا المَعْنَى.  
>  
> وَلَنْ يَنَالَ العَبْدُ الطَّبَقَةَ العَالِيَةَ إِلَّا أَنْ يُجَاوِزَ الطَّبَقَةَ السَّافِلَةَ، فَلَا يَصِلُ إِلَى طَهَارَةِ السِّرِّ مَا لَمْ يَفْرُغْ مِنْ طَهَارَةِ القَلْبِ، وَلَنْ يَصِلَ إِلَى ذَلِكَ مَنْ لَمْ يَفْرُغْ عَنْ طَهَارَةِ الجَوَارِحِ عَنِ المَنَاهِي. وَكُلَّمَا عَزَّ المَطْلُوبُ وَشَرُفَ، صَعُبَ مَسْلَكُهُ وَطَالَ طَرِيقُهُ وَكَثُرَتْ عَقَبَاتُهُ.

#### Master AQL Translation:
> Purification in every single tier constitutes exactly one-half of the operation assigned to it. For the supreme goal in the operation of the inmost secret is the unveiling of the Majesty and Grandeur of Allāh; yet authentic experiential knowledge of Allāh can never reside within the secret until everything other than Allāh departs from it!  
>  
> Therefore Allāh, Mighty and Majestic, declared: *“Say: Allāh! Then leave them to play in their vain discourse”* (Qur'an 6:91), because the Creator and the created cannot simultaneously occupy the core of the soul: *“Allāh has not made for any man two hearts within his breast”* (Qur'an 33:4).  
>  
> As for the operation of the heart, its ultimate goal is flourishing through praiseworthy virtues and revealed convictions; yet it can never be qualified by them until it is cleansed of their diametric opposites: corrupt conjectures and repugnant vices. Thus, purifying the vessel is the first indispensable half, serving as the prerequisite condition for the second half (illumination). In this exact mathematical sense, *“Purification is half of faith.”*  
>  
> And the servant can never attain the higher station until he traverses the lower: he can never reach the purity of the inmost secret until he completes the purity of the heart; and he can never reach the purity of the heart until he completes the purity of the physical limbs from forbidden deeds. Whenever the quest is sublime, its ascent is rigorous, its journey prolonged, and its trials manifold.
