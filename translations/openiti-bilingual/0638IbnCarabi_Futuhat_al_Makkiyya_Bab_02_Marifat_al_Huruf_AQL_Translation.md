**In the Name of Allāh, the Infinitely Boundless in Originating Mercy, the Continuously Dispensing of Sustaining Grace.**

### Part I: The Metaphysical Leadership of the Letters (أَئِمَّةُ الأَلْفَاظِ)

#### النَّصُّ الْعَرَبِيُّ الأَصِيلُ:
> اعْلَمْ أَنَّ هَذَا البَابَ عَلَى ثَلَاثَةِ فُصُولٍ: الفَصْلُ الأَوَّلُ فِي مَعْرِفَةِ الحُرُوفِ، وَالفَصْلُ الثَّانِي فِي مَعْرِفَةِ الحَرَكَاتِ الَّتِي تَتَمَيَّزُ بِهَا الكَلِمَاتُ، وَالفَصْلُ الثَّالِثُ فِي مَعْرِفَةِ العِلْمِ وَالعَالِمِ وَالمَعْلُومِ.  
>  
> إِنَّ الحُرُوفَ أَئِمَّةُ الأَلْفَاظِ ••• شَهِدَتْ بِذَلِكَ أَلْسُنُ الحُفَّاظِ  
> دَارَتْ بِهَا الأَفْلَاكُ فِي مَلَكُوتِهِ ••• بَيْنَ النِّيَامِ الخُرْسِ وَالأَيْقَاظِ  
> أَلْحَظَتْهَا الأَسْمَاءُ مِنْ مَكْنُونِهَا ••• فَبَدَتْ تُعِزُّ لِذَلِكَ الأَلْحَاظِ  
> وَتَقُولُ لَوْلَا فَيْضُ جُودِي مَا بَدَتْ ••• عِنْدَ الكَلَامِ حَقَائِقُ الأَلْفَاظِ  
>  
> اعْلَمْ — أَيَّدَنَا اللَّهُ وَإِيَّاكَ — أَنَّهُ لَمَّا كَانَ الوُجُودُ مُطْلَقًا مِنْ غَيْرِ تَقْيِيدٍ يَتَضَمَّنُ المُكَلِّفَ وَهُوَ الحَقُّ تَعَالَى، وَالمُكَلَّفِينَ وَهُمُ العَالَمُ؛ وَكَانَتِ الحُرُوفُ جَامِعَةً لِمَا ذَكَرْنَا: أَرَدْنَا أَنْ نُبَيِّنَ مَقَامَ المُكَلِّفِ مِنْ هَذِهِ الحُرُوفِ مِنَ المُكَلَّفِينَ، مِنْ وَجْهٍ دَقِيقٍ مُحَقَّقٍ لَا يَتَبَدَّلُ عِنْدَ أَهْلِ الكَشْفِ إِذَا وَقَفُوا عَلَيْهِ.

#### Master AQL Translation:
> Know that this chapter comprises three fundamental sections: The First Section concerns the knowledge of the Letters; The Second Section concerns the vowel motions (*Al-Ḥarakāt*) through which words are articulated and differentiated; The Third Section concerns Knowledge, the Knower, and the Known.  
>  
> *Verily, the Letters are the sovereign imams of articulated speech!*  
> *Unto this truth have the tongues of the preservers borne witness.*  
> *With them have the celestial spheres revolved within His Kingdom,*  
> *Between the slumbering mute and the awakened knowers.*  
> *The Divine Names glanced upon them from their hidden sanctuary,*  
> *Whereupon they shone forth, exalting those spiritual glances,*  
> *Saying: 'Were it not for the outpouring of my grace, there would never*  
> *Have appeared in utterance the inner realities of words!'*  
>  
> Know—may Allāh support us and you—that since existence in its absolute unconditional reality encompasses both the One who charges with obligation (the Divine Truth, Exalted is He) and those who are charged (the cosmos); and since the Arabic Letters encompass the entirety of this manifestation: we intended to elucidate the station of the Commander and the commanded within these letters from an exact, verified perspective that never wavers among the folk of spiritual unveiling when they contemplate it.

### Part II: The Four Ranks and the 261 Celestial Spheres (مَرَاتِبُ الحُرُوفِ وَالأَفْلَاكِ)

#### النَّصُّ الْعَرَبِيُّ الأَصِيلُ:
> وَلَمَّا كُوشِفْنَا عَلَى بَسَائِطِ الحُرُوفِ، وَجَدْنَاهَا عَلَى أَرْبَعِ مَرَاتِبَ:  
>  
> **حُرُوفٌ مَرْتَبَتُهَا سَبْعَةُ أَفْلَاكٍ**: وَهِيَ الأَلِفُ وَالزَّايُ وَاللَّامُ.  
> **وَحُرُوفٌ مَرْتَبَتُهَا ثَمَانِيَةُ أَفْلَاكٍ**: وَهِيَ النُّونُ وَالصَّادُ وَالضَّادُ.  
> **وَحُرُوفٌ مَرْتَبَتُهَا تِسْعَةُ أَفْلَاكٍ**: وَهِيَ العَيْنُ وَالغَيْنُ وَالسِّينُ وَالشِّينُ.  
> **وَحُرُوفٌ مَرْتَبَتُهَا عَشَرَةُ أَفْلَاكٍ**: وَهِيَ بَاقِي حُرُوفِ المُعْجَمِ (ثَمَانِيَةَ عَشَرَ حَرْفًا).  
>  
> فَكَانَ مَجْمُوعُ الأَفْلَاكِ الَّتِي عَنْهَا وُجِدَتْ هَذِهِ الحُرُوفُ البَسَائِطُ مِائَتَيْنِ وَوَاحِدًا وَسِتِّينَ فَلَكًا (261 فَلَكًا).  
>  
> أَمَّا المَرْتَبَةُ السَّبْعِيَّةُ: فَالزَّايُ وَاللَّامُ طَبْعُهُمَا الحَرَارَةُ وَاليُبُوسَةُ؛ وَأَمَّا الأَلِفُ فَطَبْعُهَا الحَرَارَةُ وَالرُّطُوبَةُ وَاليُبُوسَةُ وَالبُرُودَةُ، تَرْجِعُ مَعَ الحَارِّ حَارَّةً، وَمَعَ الرَّطْبِ رَطْبَةً، وَمَعَ البَارِدِ بَارِدَةً، وَمَعَ اليَابِسِ يَابِسَةً، بِحَسَبِ مَا تُجَاوِرُهُ مِنَ العَوَالِمِ!  
>  
> وَأَمَّا المَرْتَبَةُ الثَّمَانِيَةُ: فَحُرُوفُهَا حَارَّةٌ يَابِسَةٌ.  
> وَأَمَّا المَرْتَبَةُ التِّسْعِيَّةُ: فَالعَيْنُ وَالغَيْنُ طَبْعُهُمَا البُرُودَةُ وَاليُبُوسَةُ، وَالسِّينُ وَالشِّينُ طَبْعُهُمَا الحَرَارَةُ وَاليُبُوسَةُ.  
>  
> وَعَدَدُ الأَفْلَاكِ الَّتِي عَنْ حَرَكَتِهَا تُوجَدُ الحَرَارَةُ: مِائَتَا فَلَكٍ وَثَلَاثَةُ أَفْلَاكٍ (203).  
> وَعَدَدُ الأَفْلَاكِ الَّتِي عَنْ حَرَكَتِهَا تُوجَدُ اليُبُوسَةُ: مِائَتَا فَلَكٍ وَوَاحِدٌ وَأَرْبَعُونَ فَلَكًا (241).  
> وَعَدَدُ الأَفْلَاكِ الَّتِي عَنْ حَرَكَتِهَا تُوجَدُ البُرُودَةُ: خَمْسَةٌ وَسِتُّونَ فَلَكًا (65).  
> وَعَدَدُ الأَفْلَاكِ الَّتِي عَنْ حَرَكَتِهَا تُوجَدُ الرُّطُوبَةُ: سَبْعَةٌ وَعِشْرُونَ فَلَكًا (27).

#### Master AQL Translation:
> When the primordial elements of the Letters were unveiled unto us, we discovered them arrayed across four cosmic ranks:  
>  
> 1. **Letters of the Seven Spheres Rank**: The *Alif* (ا), the *Zāy* (ز), and the *Lām* (ل).  
> 2. **Letters of the Eight Spheres Rank**: The *Nūn* (ن), the *Ṣād* (ص), and the *Ḍād* (ض).  
> 3. **Letters of the Nine Spheres Rank**: The *ʿAyn* (ع), the *Ghayn* (غ), the *Sīn* (س), and the *Shīn* (ش).  
> 4. **Letters of the Ten Spheres Rank**: The remaining eighteen letters of the Arabic alphabet.  
>  
> Thus, the total sum of the celestial spheres from whose motion these simple primordial letters were brought into existence is exactly **261 Spheres** (*Aflāk*).  
>  
> As for the Seven-Sphere Rank: the *Zāy* and the *Lām* are intrinsically warm and dry; while the *Alif* encompasses all four primary physical natures—heat, moisture, dryness, and cold—becoming warm alongside warm entities, moist alongside moist entities, cold alongside cold entities, and dry alongside dry entities, adapting dynamically according to the cosmological realms it neighbors!  
>  
> As for the Eight-Sphere Rank: its letters (*Nūn, Ṣād, Ḍād*) are warm and dry.  
> As for the Nine-Sphere Rank: *ʿAyn* and *Ghayn* are cold and dry, while *Sīn* and *Shīn* are warm and dry.  
>  
> The distribution of celestial spheres governing thermodynamic and physical qualities:  
> • Spheres generating **Heat** through their rotational momentum: **203 Spheres**.  
> • Spheres generating **Dryness** through their rotational momentum: **241 Spheres**.  
> • Spheres generating **Cold** through their rotational momentum: **65 Spheres**.  
> • Spheres generating **Moisture** through their rotational momentum: **27 Spheres**.  
>  
> Through the interpenetration and harmonic convolution of these 261 spheres, the All-Merciful Breath (*Nafas al-Raḥmān*) condenses into the 28 phonetic stations, forming the cosmic matrix of all revelation and material existence.
